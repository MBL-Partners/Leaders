# Productivity Metrics Snapshot

A structured ten-minute scoring session that measures team output on the four
numbers that survive the arrival of AI — and shows where hours and value have
come apart.

Built by MBL Partners (https://mblpartners.com) — part of the Thrive with AI
skill library.

---

## What It Does

Scores a team across four questions designed for the post-AI reality, where
hours stop correlating with output:

1. Production — What did the team actually finish, ship, close, or decide?
2. Value Creation — Of what shipped, how much moved a real outcome versus
   cleared a queue?
3. Shipping Speed — How long did it take from started to done, and is that
   trending faster?
4. Time Cost — How much of the team's time went to meetings and admin versus
   the actual work?

From those answers, it calculates a Leverage score — value created per unit
of time spent — the one productivity number that rises when your tools improve
and your people aim higher.

A timesheet measures attendance. This skill measures contribution.

## Install

1. Download `productivity-metrics-snapshot.skill`
2. Open Cowork
3. Drag the `.skill` file into your Cowork window
4. The skill installs automatically and is ready to run

---

## How to Run It

Once installed, trigger it by saying any of the following:
- "Productivity metrics"
- "Score my team's output"
- "Measure what ships"
- "Are we measuring the right things?"

The skill asks one question at a time and waits for your response before
moving on. It takes about ten minutes.

---

## Output

A Productivity Snapshot containing:
- Scores (1–10) for each of the four dimensions with one sentence of rationale
- An overall score out of 100
- What's strong — the highest-scoring dimensions with specific evidence
- Where hours and value have come apart — named directly
- Three specific moves for next period to raise value or speed
- The Uncomfortable Read — one honest sentence on what the numbers actually show

# AI Adoption Pulse

A weekly five-minute leadership review that rates each active AI initiative
Red, Yellow, or Green, finds the one most at risk of quietly dying, and
tells you exactly where to show up this week.

Built by MBL Partners (https://mblpartners.com) — part of the Thrive with AI
skill library.

---

## What It Does

Runs a structured review of your active AI initiatives across three steps:

1. **Inventory** — lists all live initiatives where you are asking people to
   actually change how they work. If a prior pulse was saved, it pre-fills the
   list and asks what has changed.
2. **Rate each initiative** — assigns a Red, Yellow, or Green with a one-line
   friction point, one initiative at a time. For any Yellow or Red, asks the
   follow-up that matters: when the team last looked up for a signal, and
   whether you were there.
3. **The Disappearance Check** — surfaces the one initiative most likely to
   stall not because it is too hard, but because the team cannot tell whether
   leadership is still behind it.

The skill is built on the observation that adoption failures are rarely tool
failures. They are leadership disappearances.

## Install

1. Download `ai-adoption-pulse.skill`
2. Open Cowork
3. Drag the `.skill` file into your Cowork window
4. The skill installs automatically and is ready to run

---

## How to Run It

Once installed, trigger it by saying any of the following:
- "Run my adoption pulse"
- "AI adoption check"
- "Where is adoption stalling?"
- "Weekly initiative review"
- "Red yellow green review"

The skill asks one initiative at a time and waits for your rating before
moving on. It takes about five minutes. Run it weekly.

---

## Output

An AI Adoption Pulse board containing:
- A Red/Yellow/Green status board for each active initiative
- Most at risk — the single initiative most likely to die quietly this week
- Where to show up — one specific move for the week, not a general check-in
- The Stay-a-Little-Longer Note — for any Green initiative not yet spreading
  its value to others in the organization

After four saved pulses, it offers longitudinal pattern analysis: which
initiatives went Red then recovered after you showed up, and which have been
stalled for three-plus weeks — a presence problem, not a difficulty problem.
